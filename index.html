How to record equipment going out of use or coming back into use
  1. Equipment has failed or been disconnected: find it below and press Report out of use. Enter the FMS reference and the time it failed. An F3.21A is emailed to the distribution list automatically.
  2. Equipment is back in use: find the red card and press Return to service. Check the time it was reinstated. An F3.21B is emailed automatically.
  3. Made a mistake? Press Undo on the message that appears. You have 90 seconds, and nothing is emailed until 2 minutes after you confirm.
  4. Need to correct an FMS number or note later? Use Fault history → Edit. Emails that have already gone are not sent again.
